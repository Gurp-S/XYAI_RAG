Ariana Grande (real name Ariana Grande-Butera) was born and raised in Boca Raton, Fla. Her birthday is June 26, 1993, and her height is 5'2". She got her start in acting, starring in Nickelodeon sitcoms 'Victorious' (2010-2013) and 'Sam & Cat' (2013-2014). She transitioned to music in the midst of portraying Cat Valentine, launching debut album 'Yours Truly' in 2013.
