The trio have been the dominant forces for well over a decade, and it remains a hotly-debated topic when it comes to deciding which one of them is the greatest of their era, if not of all time, given they each hold various records. Federer was the first male player to reach 20 major singles titles, but in 2020 Nadal pulled level when winning the French Open once more. And then, following Federer’s retirement in 2022, Djokovic joined Nadal on 22 slams when winning a 10th Australian Open title. He made it No 23 at the French Open, and then ensured there was daylight with a record-equalling 24th in the 2023 US Open final.
