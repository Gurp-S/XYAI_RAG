Eurasia forms one single landmass, therefore, the debate on why Europe should be considered a separate continent from Asia has been going on for a long time. The Greek mariners in the 6th century B.C. were the first to name and map land and sea navigational routes and were also the first to name the two continents. From that time till now, geographers have failed to come to a clear conclusion on what really separates Europe from Asia.
