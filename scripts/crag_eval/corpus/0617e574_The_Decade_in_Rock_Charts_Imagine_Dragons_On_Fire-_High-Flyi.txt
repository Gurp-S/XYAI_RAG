It likely comes as no surprise to followers of Billboard&#x27;s rock charts that Imagine Dragons stand among the genre&#x27;s most influential artists of the decade.Twenty One Pilots‘ Blurryface rules as the No. 1 title on Billboard‘s decade-end Top Rock Albums chart. The 2015 LP led for nine total weeks, tied for sixth-most all- time, while its 234 weeks on the ranking to date are surpassed only by Lana Del Rey’s Born to Die (262) and Imagine Dragons’ Night Visions (259). Blurryface spurred a pair of smash singles, “Stressed Out” and “Ride,” which commanded Hot Rock Songs for 23 and eight weeks, respectively. After 2015 follow-up Smoke + Mirrors, 2017’s Evolve found the band back to generating multiple hit tracks, including “Believer,” “Thunder” and “Whatever It Takes.” Latest LP Origins arrived in 2018, furthering Imagine Dragons’ success across multiple radio formats and genre-based playlists. At the decade’s end, Imagine Dragons take the top three spots on Billboard‘s decade-end Hot Rock Songs list, while two of its albums adorn the top five of Top Rock Albums for the 2010s. The rankings are among 30 charts that Billboard has compiled encompassing the 2010s, including those covering all-genre streaming, radio airplay and song sales; all-genre social and touring activity; and the top songs and albums in country, R&B/hip-hop, Latin, Christian, gospel and dance/electronic.
