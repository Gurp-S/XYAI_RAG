Front Office Sports reported that in 2023, Fox has set the cost of a 30-second ad at $7 million. The price of the commercials mark the most expensive Super Bowl ad spots of all time. Back in 1967, the inaugural Super Bowl aired on CBS and NBC. It was the only year the big game was carried by two different networks. Ads sold on NBC cost $37,500, while those on CBS cost $45,500, according to SuperBowl-ads.com. That will be the case again for Super Bowl 57 between the Eagles and Chiefs. The most-watched program of the year sees companies line up each year to put together their best commercials on the TV for a massive audience. And for a lot of viewers, the ads are a main reason why people tune into the game. Getting the commercial put together is costly enough, but the price it takes to get the finished product in front of all those eyes is always expensive, as networks command a lofty price tag for these companies to air ads during the big game.
