Shareholder Overview Intellicheck, Inc., delivers on-demand digital identity validation solutions for KYC, fraud, and age verification needs. Last year, Intellicheck validated over 100 million identities both digital and physical for top financial services, fintech companies, e-commerce, retail commerce, law enforcement and government agencies across North America. Its highly accurate, fast and secure solutions are available through IDN-Direct, an API-based solution that integrates into customer systems and processes, and through IDN-Portal which can be up and running in under an hour. Intellicheck can be used through a mobile device, a browser, or a retail pos/scanner.
