SUMMIT, N.J., Sept. 14, 2023 /PRNewswire/ -- Keen Vision Acquisition Corporation ("KVAC") announced today that, commencing September 15, 2023, holders of the units sold in the Company's initial public offering completed on July 27, 2023 may elect to separately trade the ordinary shares of KVAC and the warrants in such units on The Nasdaq Global Market ("Nasdaq"). 14, 2023 /PRNewswire/ -- Keen Vision Acquisition Corporation ("KVAC") announced today that, commencing September 15, 2023, holders of the units sold in the Company's initial public offering completed on July 27, 2023 may elect to separately trade the ordinary shares of KVAC and the warrants in such units on The Nasdaq Global Market ("Nasdaq"). The ordinary shares and warrants that are separated will trade on Nasdaq under the symbols "KVAC" and "KVACW" respectively.
