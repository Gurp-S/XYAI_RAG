Check out his statistics, and the latest news.Carlos Sainz scored his debut F1 victory in 2022, but was unable to match Ferrari teammate Charles Leclerc over the course of the season. The Spaniard will enter into his fourth year with the Scuderia in 2024. Carlos Sainz Jr was destined for Formula 1 from an early age, inheriting a racing mentality from his father, two-time World Rally Champion Carlos Sainz Sr, and later securing a junior partnership with Red Bull. Sainz continued to race in the series the following year, as well as the UK Formula Renault Winter Cup, before getting a call up to race for Carlin in the British and Euro Series Formula 3 championships for 2012. Sainz's journey to his current position alongside Charles Leclerc at Ferrari has been extensive, beginning with his involvement in the karting circuit and progressing through the Red Bull junior program.
