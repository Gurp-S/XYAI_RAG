Gooey on the inside, crisp on the outside, just a couple minutes to prep, and you don’t have to turn on your oven. These chocolate chip cookies were absolutely mind-blowing to me. I couldn’t believe how quickly the air fryer pre-heated (minutes) and how crispy, BUT ALSO gooey, the cookies were. I fully plan on using this method to make single serving cookies for when the whim strikes. If the amount of time that it takes to bake your cookies in the air fryer isn’t significantly different than the amount of time it takes to bake them in the oven (and in this case, it isn’t), then why do it?! I’m here to tell you! The biggest pro to choosing the air fryer over the oven is the incredibly delicious (read: perfect) texture that the air fryer method gives the cookies – they’re ooey-gooey soft on the inside with a nice crisp texture on the outside. The ingredients are SUPER simple …you ready? All you need for air fryer chocolate chip cookies is (drumroll please!) …chocolate chip cookie dough! We chose store-bought chocolate chip cookies for this recipe, but you can absolutely make your own cookie dough. More on both options below!
